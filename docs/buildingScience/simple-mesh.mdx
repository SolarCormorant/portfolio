Solar radiation plays a critical role in building design, directly influencing a structure’s energy efficiency, thermal comfort, and sustainability. It is a key factor in decisions such as window placement, solar panel orientation, and shading system design.

Understanding the seasonal variation of solar radiation is essential for balancing heating and cooling demands throughout the year.

In summer, high solar exposure can lead to overheating in interior spaces. Well-designed and strategically placed shading systems help mitigate this effect by controlling indoor temperatures.

In winter, making use of available solar radiation supports passive heating, reducing the need for mechanical systems and lowering overall energy consumption.

For this reason, analyzing the directional and temporal distribution of solar radiation is a vital step in optimizing building performance during the design process.
