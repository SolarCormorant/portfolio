## Daylight Hours
Daylight Hours represent the periods throughout the year when a surface receives usable daylight. This analysis is used to assess daylight availability and overall daylight performance within a space.

## Solar Radiation Analysis

Solar radiation is a key driver of building performance, influencing energy use, thermal comfort, and sustainability. It informs design decisions such as glazing placement, shading strategy, and photovoltaic orientation.

Understanding seasonal variation is essential for balancing heating and cooling loads. In summer, high solar exposure can increase the risk of overheating, so well designed shading helps limit heat gains and improve comfort. In winter, capturing available solar radiation supports passive heating, reducing reliance on mechanical systems and lowering energy consumption.
