- There are two types of step covers: closed riser and open riser.


## Open Riser

- Open risers are used with Plate and Muffin step types. Cover placement on the sides is determined automatically based on the presence of structural support, while the user can optionally enable covers on the front and back of each step.

<VideoPlayer src="/video/stair/cover_open_riser.mp4" />


## Closed Riser
- Closed risers are used with step types such as Shoreline, İnci, and Reversed Step with support systems on both side. These include a specialized notch detail that simplifies installation.
<VideoPlayer src="/video/stair/cover_closed_riser.mp4" />



## Cover Unfold

- A 2D unfolded drawing of the step cover is generated dynamically alongside the steps, assisting in documentation and design evaluation.
- Only the unique steps are created then oriented to decrease computation time.
